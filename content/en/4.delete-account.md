# Delete your Worket account

You can delete your Worket account and its associated data at any time. This page explains how, which data is deleted and which is kept.

## How to delete your account

**From the app (recommended):**

1. Open Worket and sign in.
2. Go to the menu and choose **"Cancel / delete account"**.
3. Confirm the deletion. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

**Without access to the app:** email [contact@worketapp.com](mailto:contact@worketapp.com) from the address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my Worket account". We will handle your request within 30 days at most.

If you have a paid subscription, also cancel it from Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) so it does not renew.

## Data that is deleted

Your profile, photos, active offers and drafts with their chats, applications, saved items, notifications and subscription data are permanently deleted.

## Data that is kept

So as not to break other people's conversations, the text of your messages is kept in chats of already finished offers, but your identity is anonymised and shown as "Deleted user". We may keep certain information for the time strictly necessary to meet legal obligations or handle potential claims.

More information in our [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy-policy).
